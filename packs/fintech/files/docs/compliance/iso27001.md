# ISO/IEC 27001 readiness checklist

Not a certification and not advice. Certification is issued by an accredited certification body after auditing your
information security management system (ISMS). Control names follow ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A themes; read the standard
itself for the exact wording. See `DISCLAIMER.md`.

ISMS (clauses 4-10), the part teams forget:

- [ ] Scope statement: which products, sites and data the ISMS covers.
- [ ] Risk assessment method and register; risk treatment plan; Statement of Applicability (which Annex A controls apply
      and why not the others).
- [ ] Security objectives, management commitment, roles, internal audit, management review, corrective actions.

Annex A themes, with where engineering evidence comes from:

- [ ] Organisational (policies, roles, supplier security, threat intelligence, incident management, legal and
      contractual requirements, privacy and PII protection, use of AI tools).
- [ ] People (screening, training, remote work, reporting events).
- [ ] Physical (offices, equipment, secure disposal).
- [ ] Technological: access rights and MFA, privileged access, secure authentication, backup, logging and monitoring,
      vulnerability management, configuration management, data masking, DLP, secure coding, security testing in
      development, separation of environments, change management, protection of test data, deletion of information.

Engineering evidence in this project: PR history and review gates, `deno task check`, `sast`, `audit`, `secrets`, SBOM,
audit trail, retention rules (`docs/compliance/data-retention.md`), ADRs for security decisions (`docs/adr/`).
