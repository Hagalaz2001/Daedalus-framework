- Before adding analytics, tracking, a third-party SDK, an AI feature or personal data collection, check
  `docs/compliance/eu/`. Ask the owner which legal basis applies; never decide it yourself.
- Collect the minimum personal data, state the purpose, and keep a retention rule for it. No dark patterns in consent
  banners: refusing must be as easy as accepting.
- AI features that affect people (hiring, credit, education, health, biometrics) may be regulated by the EU AI Act: flag
  them for human legal review before building.
- Accessibility is a legal requirement for many consumer products in the EU: keyboard, contrast, labels, screen reader.
