# Data protection impact assessment (DPIA): <feature>

Date: | Owner (human): | DPO consulted: yes / no

1. **Description**: what is processed, why, how, which systems and processors, expected volume.
2. **Data**: categories (identifiers, contact, health, location, biometrics, children's data), special categories,
   sources.
3. **Lawful basis** and, for special categories, the Art. 9 condition.
4. **Necessity and proportionality**: why this data, why not less, retention, who has access.
5. **Rights**: how access, erasure, objection and consent withdrawal work in the product.
6. **Risks to people** (not to the company): unauthorised access, discrimination, loss of control, re-identification,
   wrongful automated decisions. For each: likelihood, severity, measures, residual risk.
7. **Measures**: encryption, pseudonymisation, access control, logging, minimisation, human review, testing.
8. **Outcome**: proceed / proceed with measures / consult the supervisory authority (Art. 36) / do not proceed.
9. **Review date** and change triggers.
