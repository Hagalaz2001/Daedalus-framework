# GDPR basics for product teams

- [ ] Map personal data: what, why, where stored, who sees it, how long (records of processing, Art. 30).
- [ ] One lawful basis per purpose (consent, contract, legal obligation, legitimate interest...). Consent must be
      opt-in, granular, withdrawable; cookies and trackers that are not strictly necessary need consent (ePrivacy).
- [ ] Privacy notice in plain language: who you are, purposes, bases, recipients, transfers, retention, rights, contact.
- [ ] Data subject rights flows: access, export, rectification, erasure, objection; one month to answer.
- [ ] Processors (hosting, email, analytics, AI APIs): data processing agreement; transfers outside the EEA need SCCs or
      an adequacy decision.
- [ ] Security (Art. 32): encryption, access control, backups, testing; breach process: authority within 72 hours.
- [ ] Children: age-appropriate design; parental consent thresholds differ by country (13-16).
- [ ] DPIA for high-risk processing (`dpia-template.md`). Privacy by design: default to the least data.
- [ ] No personal data in AI prompts unless the provider is an approved processor.
