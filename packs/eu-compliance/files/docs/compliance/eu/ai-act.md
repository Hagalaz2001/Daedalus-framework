# EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) checklist

Phased application from 2025 to 2027; the Commission has proposed changes to some dates. Check the current timetable.

- [ ] Classify each AI feature: prohibited practices (e.g. social scoring, manipulative techniques, some biometric
      uses), high-risk (Annex III areas: hiring, education, credit, essential services, law enforcement, migration,
      justice, biometrics, critical infrastructure; and AI as a safety component of regulated products), limited-risk
      (transparency duties), minimal-risk.
- [ ] Transparency: tell people when they talk to an AI system and label AI-generated or manipulated content (deepfakes,
      synthetic text on public-interest topics) where required.
- [ ] High-risk systems need: risk management, data governance, technical documentation, logging, human oversight,
      accuracy and robustness, conformity assessment, registration. These are heavy; get specialist help early.
- [ ] If you integrate a general-purpose model (GPAI) from a provider, record the provider's documentation and your own
      use; model providers have their own duties.
- [ ] AI literacy: staff who operate AI systems need adequate training (applies already).
- [ ] Keep a register of AI systems you build or use, the owner, the purpose and the risk class (`docs/adr/` or a
      table).
- [ ] Human oversight in practice: a named human can understand, override and stop the system
      (`docs/RESPONSIBLE-AI.md`).
