# European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882) checklist

Applies since 28 June 2025 to many consumer products and services: e-commerce, banking services for consumers, e-books,
passenger transport services, electronic communications, audiovisual media services. Micro-enterprises providing
services have an exemption. Member states transposed it with local details.

- [ ] Conform to WCAG 2.2 level AA (the harmonised standard EN 301 549 is based on it) for web and mobile apps.
- [ ] Keyboard-only operation, visible focus, logical order; screen reader names and roles; text alternatives.
- [ ] Contrast at least 4.5:1 (text) and 3:1 (UI); resizable text to 200%; no information by colour alone.
- [ ] Touch targets large enough; no motion or timing traps; captions for video; accessible forms and errors.
- [ ] Accessibility statement published, with a feedback contact and the date of the last review.
- [ ] Test with a screen reader and by keyboard on every release (the `a11y` and `design-qa` skills help; a human
      checks).
