# eu-compliance pack (status: starter, documents only)

Checklists for shipping to people in the EU/EEA. They name the rule, the question to ask and where it touches the code.
They are not legal advice, they will be out of date (check the dates in the official texts, several were being amended
when this was written), and following them is not compliance. See `DISCLAIMER.md`. Ask a lawyer who specialises in EU
digital law.

Why this pack exists: GDPR, the AI Act, the Cyber Resilience Act and the Accessibility Act catch ordinary web and mobile
products, and AI-assisted teams ship fast enough to miss them.
