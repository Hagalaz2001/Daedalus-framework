A weekly review does not need a ritual. It needs ten minutes and a rule: **touch every note once.**

## The routine

1. Open the notes from the last seven days.
2. Delete what no longer matters.
3. Copy anything unfinished into a single list titled "Next week".
4. Pick the three that would make the week a good one.

## Why it works

Notes pile up because capture is cheap and revisiting is not. A fixed, short review keeps the cost of revisiting low enough that you actually do it.

If a note survives three reviews without action, it was not a task. Keep it as reference or let it go.
